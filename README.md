## 🌟 Overview

**AlumniX** is a modern web platform designed to bridge the gap between students and alumni of educational institutions. Using AI-powered matching, it helps students find the perfect mentors, discover relevant job opportunities, and get career advice — all while enabling alumni to give back to their alma mater.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 For Students
- 🎯 **AI-Powered Mentor Matching** — Get matched with alumni based on your skills, interests, and career goals
- 💼 **Curated Opportunities** — Internships and jobs ranked by relevance to your profile
- 🤖 **AI Career Advisor** — Chat with an AI advisor for guidance and recommendations
- 📊 **Profile Match Score** — Track how well your profile matches with mentors and opportunities
- ✨ **AI Bio Generator** — Auto-generate professional bios based on your skills and interests

### 🎓 For Alumni
- 📬 **Connection Requests** — Receive and manage student mentorship requests
- 💼 **Post Opportunities** — Share job openings and internships from your company
- 🏆 **Mentor Score** — Build your reputation as a mentor (visible to recruiters)
- 📊 **Impact Tracking** — See how many students you've helped get hired
- 🎯 **Smart Student Discovery** — Find students who align with your expertise

- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)

- **State Management:** React Hooks (useState, useEffect)
